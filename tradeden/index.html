GOOD TO KNOW

A few straight answers.

Is Trade Den available now?

We’re preparing the launch. Email us for updates; we’ll share the download destination when it is ready.

Does Trade Den trade for me?

No. The current experience is for market research, learning, alerts and paper simulation. It does not submit live broker orders.

Can I try my own strategies?

Yes. Trade Den includes strategy building and historical research tools so you can inspect a rule and how it behaved on saved market data. Historical results do not predict future outcomes.
