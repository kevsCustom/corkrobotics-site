Small-business customization

Your artwork.
At work.

For small brands and packaging creators, Cork V1 offers a portable way to explore drawing a saved design onto a small batch. Its phone app’s AI prepares the scanned surfaces and artwork placement. You review the plot before the robot draws with its pen.

A small-batch example

One saved logo. Six boxes.

Imagine preparing a small run of packaging with the same logo. In Cork V1’s current phone workflow, you can ask the AI to retrieve a saved logo and place it on six boxes in front of the machine. It scans the workspace, meshes the surfaces and prepares the artwork placement.

You review and accept the prepared plot before Cork V1 draws. That review gives you a chance to check the intended layout across the batch. See the complete preparation sequence for what happens at each stage.

The task begins with your existing artwork. A clear, simple logo is a useful first experiment because it makes the placement and the resulting pen marks easier to inspect.

Before a batch

Evaluate one sample first.

A box’s finish affects what a pen leaves behind. Start with a spare sample of the exact packaging you intend to use. Look at how the ink behaves, whether the lines remain readable at your chosen size, and how the material’s texture affects the result.

  • Choose the artwork and identify the area you want to mark.
  • Check one pen-and-surface combination before committing saleable packaging.
  • Review the prepared placement on each item, including margins and orientation.
  • Inspect the finished sample before preparing the rest of the batch.

Keep a record of the pen, packaging and artwork size that you evaluate. A successful sample answers a specific project question; it does not establish compatibility with every box or coating.

Room for other work

Bring it out for the job.

A small business often shares one table between several tasks. Cork V1’s folding configuration, carry handle and transport case let the robot travel to your workspace. It runs on its integrated battery or plugged-in power, without a permanent bench installation.

When assessing fit, think about your actual batch size, the time you can spend preparing and inspecting a job, and the finish you want on the packaging. Cork V1’s current pen workflow is a way to explore physical customization; your own sample should guide the decision.
