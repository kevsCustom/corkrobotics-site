Installation

Download, open, and sign in.

  1. 01

    Extract the download

    On Windows, right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All. Keep the entire extracted folder together. On Mac, open the ZIP and drag MarketApp Pen into Applications.

  2. 02

    Open Bull Pen

    On Windows, open MarketApp Pen.exe inside the extracted folder. On Mac, open MarketApp Pen from Applications.

  3. 03

    Connect your account

    Choose Sign in to your MarketApp account. In the phone app, go to Account → Link a computer and enter the displayed code to access your saved stock history.

What is included

More room to review your research.

Swing trading preset

Choose 30-minute, 1-hour or 2-hour bars, with overnight holding and trading-session holding limits.

Expanded leaderboard

Give the leaderboard the main window and keep the animated traders in a smaller view. Swap them whenever you like.

Trader exports and recovery

Save sortable spreadsheets and importable trader profiles. When signed in, completed runs can also save a recovery copy to MarketApp. Keep the app open until saving finishes.

This is research software. Windows 0.2.5 supports multiple replay workers. Fastest leaves one logical processor for coordination and budgets up to 80% of RAM; large histories and other apps can reduce the worker count. Alternative cloud providers and multi-computer runs are still in development. No API keys or signed-in account are included in either download.
