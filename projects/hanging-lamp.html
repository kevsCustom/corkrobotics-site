Projects/Hanging lamp

Hanging
lamp.

From a towel to a pendant.

A sculptural shade inspired by draped fabric. Vertical ribs trace the folds, and a threaded neck screws onto a two-piece clamp ring around the pendant fitting.

Download STL files Download 3MF print plates

Five STL files · 133.9 MB ZIP · Two 3MF plates · 43.9 MB ZIP

Print the folds.

The STL download includes both clamp halves, the shade on its own, a separate support mesh, and the shade with modeled breakaway supports. Choose the shade version that suits your print setup.

Shade
Approximately 240 × 249 mm, with a 164 mm drop. Check your printer’s usable build area before slicing.
Printing
Print the shade upright, hem down, and the clamp halves on their split faces. The supported shade includes modeled breakaway ribs; remove these after printing. Review the supplied PLA plates in your slicer for your printer and filament.
Hardware
Two M3 × 10 socket-head screws and two M3 heat-set inserts, plus your pendant fitting and LED bulb.

Check the fit.

This clamp is shaped for the original pendant fitting. Measure your fitting before printing.

Clamp
Designed around a 46.6 mm diameter, 6.5 mm tall pendant band, just above its knurled ring.
Assembly
Fit the heat-set inserts into the right clamp half, secure both halves around the fitting with the screws, then screw the shade onto the clamp ring by hand.
Bulb
Designed around an ST64 E26 LED bulb, approximately 64 × 140 mm. Use an LED bulb only, check clearance and operating temperature against your filament’s limits, and keep the shade clear of the bulb. Do not use incandescent or halogen bulbs.
