Before you print.

Five printed parts and two steel pins bring the stand together.

Print
PLA, designed without supports. A 3MF plate includes the part orientations; choose settings for your printer and filament.
Hardware
2 mm steel rod, cut to 44 mm and 28.8 mm. Allow 75 mm of stock for cutting and finishing.
Fit
Designed around an iPhone 16 measuring about 14 mm thick including its case and a ¾-inch (19.05 mm) tabletop. Adapt the STEP file for other sizes.

The styled edition is supplied for printing and refinement. Its print fit has not yet been verified; socket and thread clearances may need tuning.

The guide walks through each part, pin and fit check. Print it at 100% to use the true-size sorting card.
