CorkBot launch email list · Updated October 9, 2026

This notice explains how Cork Robotics handles information you share when joining the CorkBot launch email list.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your email address and your consent to receive CorkBot product updates and launch news. Our email provider keeps subscription and confirmation records, including whether you have confirmed or unsubscribed.

We may also record the campaign source included in the signup link, such as Instagram or YouTube, to understand which campaigns bring people to CorkBot. The launch page does not save your email address in your browser.

How we use it

We use your email to send a signup confirmation, CorkBot product updates, and news of the planned Kickstarter launch. Joining the list is free. It does not purchase or reserve a CorkBot, or require a future pledge.

Who processes it

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes signup requests. An email service provider, Brevo or MailerLite, stores and manages the mailing list, confirmation emails, campaign delivery, and unsubscribe requests. The signup form identifies the provider when it uses a hosted form. We do not maintain a separate Cloudflare database of newsletter subscribers.

If a Cloudflare Turnstile security check is enabled, Cloudflare receives information needed to verify the signup request, including your IP address. We do not pass your IP address to the email provider through our website’s signup endpoint. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information

Every marketing email will include an unsubscribe link. You can also contact hello@corkrobotics.com to ask about your subscription or request deletion. We may retain a suppression record to respect your unsubscribe request and avoid adding you back by mistake.

We keep subscription information while it is needed to manage the launch list and your preferences. We will review it when the campaign ends; contact us if you would like it removed sooner.

Optional VIP reservations

VIP reservations are being prepared. Before payments open, the offer will include its own reservation and refund terms. Any enabled checkout will be hosted by Stripe, which handles payment information. This website does not collect card details. A payment receipt address does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

Contact

For questions about this list or your information, contact Cork Robotics at hello@corkrobotics.com.
