Portable. Battery powered. CorkBot.

Meet CorkBot. A portable drawing robot.

A portable robot for drawing, making, and hands-on exploration. Take it in its case. Power it by battery or plug it in. Prepare your next project with the phone app’s AI.

The robot

Start with a pen.
Take it from there.

CorkBot brings robotic motion to wherever you make things. Its folding arm and carry handle keep it compact between projects, ready to travel in its case.

Start with the pen tool and a drawing. The integrated battery gives you a portable setup, with plug-in power when you want it. More tools and bases are in development around the same core.

A small footprint.
A useful working area.

Designed for the workbench, with an aluminum structure, integrated battery, and a shared mounting interface for tools.

Phone app + AI

Ask. Review.
Make.

One request starts the preparation. You approve the plot before CorkBot gets to work.

  1. Ask for the job.

    “Use my saved logo on these six boxes.”

  2. Let the AI prepare it.

    The phone app retrieves your artwork, scans and meshes the surfaces, then places the drawing.

  3. Review and accept.

    Check the prepared plot in the app and accept it. Then CorkBot begins.
