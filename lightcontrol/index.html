01 · Install

Install the firmware

Connect and install

Your browser can't install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer.

Installing needs a secure (https) page.

Plug the ESP32-C3 into this computer with a data USB cable, press the button and pick the port that appears. Takes about a minute. Works in Chrome and Edge on desktop.

Firmware 1.0.0 for ESP32-C3.

02 · App

Get the app

Coming soon to the App Store

The free lightControl LED iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store. Once installed, open it and your light appears under Lights. On first power-up the light glows warm white so you know the wiring works.

03 · LED Setup

Tell it about your LEDs

  1. In the app, open your light, then LED Setup.
  2. Enter how many LEDs your strip has. The light restarts to apply it.
  3. Tap Test colours. The first LED should be red, the second green and the third blue. If not, change the colour order.
  4. Choose Loop if the strip forms a closed shape, or Strip for a single run.
  5. Step the start LED until the lit LED sits where you want bars and sunrises to begin, such as the bottom middle of a square.
  6. Set the power limit to what your 5 V supply can deliver.

04 · Parts

What you need

  • ESP32-C3 board (any with native USB, such as the ESP32-C3 SuperMini)
  • WS2812B LED strip (up to 600 LEDs)
  • INMP441 I2S microphone (only for Bar Spectrum)
  • 5 V power supply sized for your strip: about 60 mA per LED at full white
  • 330 Ω resistor for the LED data line

Power long strips from the supply, not through the board's USB. A computer's USB port gives about 500 mA, enough for roughly 8 LEDs at full white.

06 · Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi is optional

The light works over Bluetooth alone. Give it Wi-Fi in the app only if you schedule sunrises, so it keeps the right time after a power cut.
