lightControl · Privacy

No data collected.

The lightControl app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

01 · Scope

About this policy

This policy covers the lightControl iPhone app, the lightControl firmware that runs on your light, and the lightControl firmware installer on this website.

02 · Collection

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not connect to the internet and has no servers to send data to. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code in the app. We never see your settings, your Wi-Fi details or how you use your light.

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide crash reports for the app under Apple's own privacy policy. That is controlled by Apple and your iPhone's settings, not by the app.

03 · On your phone

What stays on your phone

  • The Wi-Fi network name and password you type in the Wi-Fi tab. The password is kept in the iOS Keychain, on this iPhone only. The network name is kept in the app's local preferences.
  • App preferences, such as the last mode you used.

None of this leaves your phone except as described in the next section.

04 · To your light

What goes to your light

Over Bluetooth, the app sends your light its settings and the current time. It sends your Wi-Fi name and password only when you tap the button to send your saved Wi-Fi to that light. The light stores them in its own memory and never sends the password back.

Your light uses Wi-Fi only to keep its clock correct for Sunrise schedules, by asking a public internet time server (pool.ntp.org) for the time. Nothing else is sent anywhere.

05 · Bluetooth

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find and control lightControl lights near you. It looks only for lightControl lights and does not list or connect to other Bluetooth devices.

06 · Web installer

The firmware installer

The installer runs in your browser. It loads the open-source ESP Web Tools script from unpkg.com and writes the firmware to your board over USB. Nothing from your board or computer is uploaded to us.

07 · Your choices

Removing your information

  • In the app's Wi-Fi tab, Forget saved Wi-Fi removes the network name and password from your phone.
  • On a light's screen, Forget Wi-Fi on this light removes them from that light.
  • Factory reset erases everything saved on a light, including its Wi-Fi, and restarts it.
  • You can turn off Bluetooth access for lightControl at any time in iOS Settings.

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
