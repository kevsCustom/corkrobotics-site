How it works

Ask. Review.
Make.

In its current pen configuration, Cork V1 works as a portable pen plotter, guiding a physical pen along prepared paths. The phone app’s AI prepares the job from your saved artwork. You review and accept the plot before drawing begins. Here is how that request becomes a physical drawing.

The phone workflow

From a saved logo to a physical drawing.

This sequence explains the current AI preparation workflow. The example request uses artwork you have already saved.

“Use my saved logo on the six boxes in front of Cork V1.”

Example request
  1. 01

    Retrieve

    The app’s AI retrieves your saved logo for the job.

  2. 02

    Scan

    The system scans the workspace and the surfaces in front of the robot.

  3. 03

    Mesh

    It builds a surface mesh: a geometric representation used to prepare the drawing.

  4. 04

    Place

    The AI places the artwork and prepares the plot across the scanned surfaces.

  5. 05

    You review

    You review and accept the prepared plot before the robot starts drawing.

  6. 06

    Draw

    Cork V1 executes the accepted plot with the physical pen.

Your part in the process

Prepare the project. Check the plan.

You supply the artwork and the physical items you want to work with. For a first project, choose one surface and a simple design so you can inspect the pen marks before preparing a batch.

At the review stage, look at the intended artwork, its scale and its position on each item. This is the decision point between a digital plan and a physical job. The AI handles the preparation sequence; your acceptance starts the plot.

The small-business guide covers batch preparation. The art and making guide looks at line work and material experiments.

A setup you can move

Carry the robot to the project.

Cork V1 folds into a compact configuration with a carry handle and a case for transport. Integrated battery power and plug-in operation give you two ways to power the machine.

The current pen configuration brings robotic drawing to a work surface without a permanent bench installation. Choose a stable workspace with room for the robot and the project, and keep the physical layout consistent with the job you review.
